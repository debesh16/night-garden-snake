    How to play
    
    
      
      
    • Move with arrow keys or WASD, swipe, or the on-screen pad. Two quick presses inside one step are both kept.
      • 
      
    • Hex board: six keys steer:Q ↖, W ↗, E →, D ↘, S ↙, A ←. Arrow keys also work.
      • 
      
    • Rewind: hold R or Backspace. Three uses per run.
      • 
      
    • Pause: P or Esc. Mute: M.
      • 
      
    • Two players: green uses WASD, mint uses the arrow keys. Crashing into anything ends your round; heads colliding is a draw.
      • 
      
    • Fruit cycles apple, banana, grapes. Each one speeds you up a little.
